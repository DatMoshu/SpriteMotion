Poke-through

Counts body pixels that show through the item, over every frame of the checked animations in 5 directions. The first run is the baseline.

How poke pixels are counted

Body faces that show in front of the item by more than the renderer's allowance: the 1 cm holdout margin (or the Hide body inward distance, if larger, in clothing mode) plus the 6 mm push-out for skinned garments (not rigid, helm, weapon, shield, bow, quiver). Clothing counts limb and head faces under the item; body counts the whole body; none counts nothing. These are the holes the renderer would cut. Compare counts; don't read them as absolutes.
